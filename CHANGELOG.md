# Changelog

## 1.2.0 — 2026-10-24

- **Mine at normal speed while riding** (new option, on by default): break blocks from your ghast
  as fast as standing on the ground, instead of 5 times slower.
- **Pass through entities** (new option, on by default): the ghast you fly glides through boats
  and other ghasts and no longer pushes mobs away.
- **First-person visibility:** choose how your ghast and its cargo (hanging boats, whoever sits in
  them, mobs on its leads) look in first person: Normal, Hidden or Semi-transparent. Semi-transparent
  fades the harness too. Replaces the old "Hide ghast in first person" option, keeping your choice.
- **Riding with two:** the pilot keeps control when the other rider gets off or stands on top of
  the ghast.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-16

- **Hang a boat from your ghast:** while riding, right-click your ghast with a boat in hand and a
  lead in your inventory, and a boat hangs below it, ready to carry friends or mobs (can be turned
  off).
- **Build through your ghast** (new option, off by default): place blocks where your ghast's body
  is, so it no longer gets in the way when you build looking down.
- **Vanilla controls option:** fly the way the base game does (look up or down to climb, Sneak
  gets you off) while keeping pass-through blocks, calling and the other features.
- **Calling your ghast** now stops it a few blocks away instead of right next to you; if it is
  already close, it just turns to face you.
- **Hiding the ghast in first person** now also hides the boats hanging from it and their ropes.
- **Descending with Sneak** no longer crouches you: the camera stays steady.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-13

First release for Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric). The Happy Ghast as it always should have been: a little bit of creative inside survival, to build comfortably anywhere.

- Ride a harnessed Happy Ghast like creative flight, with smooth, adjustable acceleration.
- Pass through blocks to stay with your ghast while building in the air, inside mountains or in
  tight spaces (can be turned off).
- Speed presets: faster than vanilla, but much slower than elytra by default; Vanilla, Calm,
  Recommended, Fast, Creative or your own.
- Call your ghast back with a key (`G` by default).
- Choose what your ghast does when you get off: float around naturally, stay still or wander
  like vanilla.
- Dismount with `Left Alt`, so Sneak can descend. Every key can be changed.
- Simple settings screen with no extra libraries (Mod Menu supported) and an option to hide the
  ghast in first person.
- Works on servers without the mod (flight controls only); server owners can set limits.
- Available in English, Español (España), Español (México), Português (Brasil), Português
  (Portugal), Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Nederlands, Polski, Türkçe, Русский, Українська,
  简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語 and 한국어.
